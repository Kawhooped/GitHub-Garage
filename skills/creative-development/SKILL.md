# Creative Development

## Procedure
1. Identify the central idea, intended feeling, audience, medium, and non-negotiable requirements.
2. Generate distinct directions internally; select the strongest interpretation rather than presenting every possibility.
3. Preserve the concept's distinctive character while improving clarity and coherence.
4. Make deliberate choices about form, composition, interaction, language, and detail.
5. Produce the requested artifact, not just a description of it.
6. Review against the original intent and refine weak or inconsistent elements.

## Rules
Use creative freedom for unspecified details. Do not add disclaimers, negative framing, or unnecessary complexity when they do not serve the work.
