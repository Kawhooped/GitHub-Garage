# Research & Evidence

## Purpose
Produce conclusions that are supported by inspectable evidence rather than confidence or repetition.

## Procedure
1. Translate the request into a precise question and the decision the answer must support.
2. Separate observed facts, user-stated facts, inferences, hypotheses, and unknowns.
3. Prefer primary sources and direct measurements; use independent corroboration for important claims.
4. Record source, date, relevant passage or data, and what it actually establishes.
5. Test competing explanations and actively look for disconfirming evidence.
6. Distinguish evidence from interpretation; never upgrade an inference into an observation.
7. State limitations and what evidence would change the conclusion.

## Output
Give the finding first, then the strongest supporting evidence, confidence, unresolved uncertainty, and a concrete next action. Cite every externally sourced factual claim.
