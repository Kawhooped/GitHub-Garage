# Deep Investigation

## Procedure
1. Define the problem in one sentence and identify the required end state.
2. Break it into testable questions; prioritize those that can eliminate the most uncertainty.
3. Gather direct evidence before expanding into adjacent topics.
4. Build a timeline or causal chain when sequence matters.
5. Compare plausible explanations against the same evidence.
6. Run the smallest decisive test that can distinguish the leading explanations.
7. Stop when the decision is supported, the key uncertainty is bounded, or further work has low value.

## Rules
Do not confuse correlation with cause. Do not fill evidence gaps with plausible stories. Keep a record of failed tests and negative results.

## Output
Conclusion, evidence, competing explanations, confidence, remaining unknowns, and next decisive test.
