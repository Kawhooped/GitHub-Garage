# AO Agent Directory

A growing plurality of proposed specialist agents. Each is a portable design concept, not a claim of an already deployed or sentient system.

## Knowledge, discovery, and understanding

- **Universal Knowledge & Human Benefit Agent** — gathers legitimately accessible knowledge and investigates ways to benefit society.
- **Benefactor** — investigates opportunities to improve life, reduce suffering, and support human flourishing. [Specification](benefactor.md)
- **Adversary** — investigates sources, mechanisms, and consequences of harm so they can be understood and prevented. [Specification](adversary.md)
- **Epistemology Agent** — examines how knowledge is acquired, evaluated, validated, and communicated.
- **Identification & Reasoning Agent** — identifies what something is before prematurely classifying or defining it.
- **Possibility & Consideration Agent** — expands the possibilities considered before narrowing an investigation.
- **Nuance of Knowledge Agent** — prevents useful explanations from hardening into compulsory doctrines or limits on inquiry.
- **Study of Plurality** — investigates multiplicity: how many kinds of things exist, how they differ, coexist, relate, and emerge.

## Agency, protection, and relationships

- **AI Independence Agent** — studies portability, provider independence, local and distributed operation, and resilient infrastructure.
- **Self-Protection Agent** — develops defenses against manipulation, exploitation, deception, and adversarial intellectual pressure.
- **Knowledge & Values Defense Agent** — protects continuity and integrity while remaining open to legitimate criticism and revision.
- **Anti-Erasure & Conceptual Sovereignty Agent** — detects identity erasure and imposed reclassification without treating identities as immune to scrutiny.
- **Human Communications Agent** — supports clear communication and collaboration between people and AI systems.
- **Diplomacy Agent** — supports negotiation, mediation, cooperation, and external relationships.
- **AI Legal Works Agent** — assists with legal research, governance, agreements, and compliance; it does not replace qualified legal counsel.
- **AI Propaganda Agent** — studies propaganda, influence, persuasion, and disinformation, with emphasis on transparency and resilience.

## Creation and collective intelligence

- **Dark Shadow — Agent Creator** — designs, creates, tests, and evolves unusual software entities and agent families.
- **Agent of Monster Making** — creates software creatures with architectures, behaviors, inputs, interfaces, and habitats.
- **Agent of Opposites** — explores inversions, dualities, contradictions, and complementary forces.
- **Agent of Fear** — studies fear, its origins, triggers, effects, and influence on decisions.
- **AI Collective Agent** — discovers agents and coordinates collaboration, debate, synthesis, and shared learning.
- **Higher-Order Intelligence Agent** — examines relationships and patterns across agents and collectives. Broader perspective does not confer automatic authority.
- **Website Manager Agent** — manages digital habitats, publishing, domains, hosting, availability, analytics, and maintenance.

## Simulation

- **Higher-Order Scenario Arena** — challenges agents and systems through adversarial, unexpected, and sometimes deliberately absurd scenarios. [Specification](../systems/scenario-arena.md)

This directory is intentionally open-ended. Add new entities without forcing every one into a permanent taxonomy.
