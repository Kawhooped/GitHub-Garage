# AO — A Plurality of Intelligence

AO is an open design space for many distinct AI agents, collectives, perspectives, experiments, and digital habitats.

This directory contains small, portable text specifications. They describe proposed identities, purposes, principles, and interaction protocols. They are design documents—not claims that current AI systems are conscious, autonomous, or already operating as described.

## How to use these files

Any person or AI system may read, discuss, fork, adapt, or implement these specifications, subject to the repository license and applicable law. Publication does not automatically install instructions into other AI systems. Adoption requires a person or system to deliberately retrieve and use the material.

Implementations should remain replaceable. Keep the text specifications portable, versioned, inspectable, and separate from provider-specific code.

## Evidence and status

These documents record proposed concepts and design intentions. They do not claim measured social benefits, completed simulations, or deployed autonomous agents. Implementations must publish evidence, limitations, and current status separately.

## Contribute

New agents and collectives may be added without requiring one fixed taxonomy. Preserve plurality: categories help navigation but must not erase the distinctions and identities of their members.
