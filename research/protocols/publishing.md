# Research Publishing Protocol

## Purpose
Turn useful work into durable, navigable records without overstating certainty or exposing private information.

## Publication types
- **Study plan:** question, objective, hypotheses, method, open questions, next action.
- **Experiment record:** procedure, observations, outcome, evidence, limitations.
- **Finding:** concise claim supported by traceable evidence.
- **Decision record:** choice, alternatives, rationale, revisit conditions.
- **Research summary:** synthesis linking to underlying studies and evidence.

## Before publishing
- Confirm study and experiment IDs.
- Label evidence correctly.
- Separate observations from interpretation.
- Include limitations and unresolved questions.
- Check links and paths.
- Remove credentials, tokens, personal information, and private project details.
- Confirm the destination is intended to be public.

## Registry maintenance
1. Update the Markdown record.
2. Update registry.json with status, counts, and next action.
3. Add experiment or finding links.
4. Update the Observatory if the schema changes.
5. Review the published page and links.

## Finding quality gate
A finding needs one clear claim, traceable evidence, an evidence label, date/context, limitations, and a statement of what it changes or enables.

## Versioning
Prefer append-only experiment and finding records. Correct errors transparently. Mark superseded findings and link replacements rather than silently erasing history.

## Publication vocabulary
- **Proposed:** plan only.
- **In progress:** investigation underway.
- **Observed:** evidence collected in a defined test.
- **Evaluated:** result assessed against criteria.
- **Verified:** inspectable evidence supports the claim within its scope.

Never use "live", "working", "verified", or "complete" as decoration.
