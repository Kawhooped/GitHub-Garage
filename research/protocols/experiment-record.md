# Experiment Record Protocol

Use this template for tests that produce evidence relevant to a registered study.

## Required fields
- **Experiment ID:** stable identifier, e.g. CS-001-E001
- **Parent study:** CS-NNN
- **Title and date/time:**
- **Environment:** tools, versions, and relevant configuration
- **Research question:**
- **Hypothesis:** falsifiable prediction, or mark exploratory
- **Method:** exact steps and inputs
- **Observations:** what happened, separate from interpretation
- **Result:** pass, fail, mixed, inconclusive, or not run
- **Evidence:** files, links, logs, screenshots, measurements
- **Limitations:** confounders and missing data
- **Interpretation:** what observations support and do not support
- **Next action:**

## Rules
1. Define success and failure conditions before testing where practical.
2. Preserve raw observations separately from interpretation.
3. Record failed and inconclusive tests.
4. Do not treat correlation as causation.
5. Do not generalize beyond tested conditions.
6. Label claims using the research charter.
7. Link findings to the experiment supporting them.
8. Preserve earlier records when revising a result.

## Outcome vocabulary
- **Pass:** predefined success conditions met.
- **Fail:** predefined success conditions not met.
- **Mixed:** some conditions met, others not.
- **Inconclusive:** evidence cannot distinguish alternatives.
- **Not run:** planned but not executed.

A written plan is not an experiment. An experiment is not automatically a verified finding.
